In progress

Trace Maps via Localizations

By Goodwillie’s theorem, the Jones–Goodwillie trace from rational K-theory to rational negative cyclic homology induces isomorphisms on relative groups for ring surjections with nilpotent kernel. Its fiber is therefore nilinvariant. I have been working to recast Cortiñas’s work characterizing the fiber of this trace map using ∞-categories and localizations, and I have recovered his characterization of the trace as the universal such map for associative, possibly nonunital rings.

Future directions

Animated Rings and Integral Trace Methods

Having recovered Cortiñas’s result for discrete rings, I am working to extend it to animated associative rings and 𝔼₁-ring spectra. A second direction is to study the other leg of the fracture square prime by prime, with the goal of proving an integral version of the result.
