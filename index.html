01 About

Algebraic K-theory and higher algebra.

My research concerns algebraic K-theory and trace methods. Noncommutative differential forms play a central role in this work, particularly in studying Hochschild and cyclic homology. I am particularly interested in deformation invariance, localization, and descent, and in how these ideas can be expressed in the language of ∞-categories.

My current work revisits Cortiñas’s infinitesimal K-theory from this perspective, focusing on infinitesimal hypercohomology, tautological characters, and Čech methods.

I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics from Union University (TN) in 2024, where I was a member of the baseball team. Before transferring to Union, I also played two years of baseball at Siena University (NY).

Work in progress

Trace Maps via Localizations

By Goodwillie’s theorem, the Jones–Goodwillie trace from rational K-theory to rational negative cyclic homology induces isomorphisms on relative groups for ring surjections with nilpotent kernel. Its fiber is therefore nilinvariant.

I have been working to recast Cortiñas’s work characterizing the fiber of this trace map using ∞-categories and localizations, and I have recovered his characterization of the trace as the universal such map for associative, possibly nonunital rings.

Related research questions

Čech methods and spectral convergence

I study Čech descriptions of infinitesimal hypercohomology and the convergence of the associated spectral sequences. A central concern is the role of totalization towers and derived inverse limits in comparisons with rational K-theory.
