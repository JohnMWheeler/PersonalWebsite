01 About

Algebraic K-theory and higher categories.

My research concerns algebraic K-theory and its relationship with Hochschild and cyclic homology. I am particularly interested in deformation invariance, localization, and descent, and in how these ideas can be expressed in the language of ∞-categories.

My current work revisits Cortiñas’s infinitesimal K-theory from this perspective, focusing on infinitesimal hypercohomology, tautological characters, and Čech methods.

02 Research

Manuscript in progress

Revisiting Infinitesimal K-Theory via Localizations

I am developing an ∞-categorical reformulation of Cortiñas’s infinitesimal hypercohomology and tautological character. The project studies their universal properties through localization and their relationship with the rational Jones–Goodwillie character.

Δ
Related research questions

Čech methods and spectral convergence

I study Čech descriptions of infinitesimal hypercohomology and the convergence of the associated spectral sequences. A central concern is the role of totalization towers and derived inverse limits in comparisons with rational K-theory.

05 Notes & reading

Topics I am working through.

Alongside my research, I write expository notes on the categorical and homological tools that enter these questions. Current topics include:

  • Hochschild and cyclic homology

    Mixed complexes, negative cyclic homology, and their connections with algebraic K-theory.

  • Descent and higher categories

    Sheaves, Čech constructions, ∞-topoi, and the distinction between a site presentation and its intrinsic categorical structure.

  • Homotopy limits and spectral sequences

    Totalization, the Bousfield–Kan spectral sequence, and the Milnor exact sequence for towers of spectra.
